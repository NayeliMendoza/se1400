My Journey in Pastry Art

✦

I’ve spent my life perfecting the craft of pastry, transforming simple ingredients into handcrafted works of art. My journey has taken me through some of the most respected bakeries in the industry, where my dedication to quality and detail quickly set my work apart.

I built a strong reputation at Harmons, known for their artisanal bakery standards, where my pastries became recognized for their consistency, elegance, and refined technique. My work became so sought after that I was recruited by a staurant in Zion, who specifically headhunted me after seeing the level of craftsmanship I brought to every creation.

Later, Harmons reached back out to bring me home because my skill and artistic approach were considered irreplaceable. After years in the corporate baking world and long commutes, I made the decision to step away and bring that same “headhunted quality” directly to my local community through Élite Sweets.

My Craft & Signature Pastries

My work is defined by precision, elegance, and a deep respect for classic French pastry techniques. Every item is handcrafted from scratch, focusing on texture, flavor, and presentation.

My éclairs and cream puffs are made with light-as-air choux pastry and filled with silky, velvety creams that melt in your mouth. Each fruit tart features a buttery, crisp shell topped with vibrant, glistening fruit that changes with the season for peak freshness.

My crème brûlée is a signature favorite—rich vanilla custard hidden beneath a perfectly caramelized sugar crust that shatters delicately with every spoonful.

Every creation is handcrafted with intention, balancing flavor and artistry to create desserts that feel as beautiful as they taste.
